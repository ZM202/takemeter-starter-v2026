For this milestone, I decided to use three labels to classify Reddit comments. I noticed that some people explain their opinions, and others make statements or just react to the topic.

1 -  Labels and Definitions

Analysis: A comment that makes a point and supports it with a specific example, fact or personal experience. 

hot_take: A comment that makes a strong statement or opinion without providing evidence to support it.

reaction: A comment that mainly expresses a feeling, such as excitement, frustration, disappointment, or surprise without making a developed argument.

2 - Examples

Analysis

Example 1: In the AI productivity discussion, one commenter mentioned working with someone whose mistakes took up half their day to fix.

Reddit comment: I would classify this as analysis because the person used a specific workplace experience to support their point.

Example 2: In the MapQuest discussion, someone explained that they hadn't used MapQuest since around 2005 but recently downloaded it and paid for an ad-free subscription.

Reddit comment: I chose analysis because the commenter gave a specific example of what they did rather than just saying they liked MapQuest.

Hot Take

Example 1: A commenter argued that stronger antitrust laws are needed because companies with good products often get bought by larger competitors.

Reddit comment: I would label this hot_take because the commenter made a strong argument without providing evidence.

Example 2: Someone commented that another person would be among the first to lose their job to AI.

Reddit comment: This is a hot_take because it's a confident prediction without supporting information.

Reaction 

Example 1: One commenter said they needed the ad-removal technology for their Samsung smart TV.

Reddit comment: I chose reaction because the person was mainly expressing frustration with their TV.

Example 2: In the AI productivity discussion, someone questioned who ever thought AI would save people.

Reddit comment: I would classify this as reaction because it was a quick response without an explanation or supporting details.

Borderline Example: The MapQuest comment about downloading the app after not using it since 2005 could fit both analysis and reaction.

The commenter was excited about MapQuest, which sounds like a reaction. However, they also shared a specific experience about downloading the app and purchasing a subscription.

Decision Rule: If a comment makes a point and provides a specific example or fact to support it, I will classify it as analysis, even if the person sounds emotional. If it makes a strong claim without support, it will be hot_take. If it mainly expresses a feeling without making an argument, it will be reaction.
